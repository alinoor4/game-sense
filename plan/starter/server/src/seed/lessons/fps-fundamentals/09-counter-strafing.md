## Why it matters

In tactical shooters your bullets only go where you aim while you are standing still, or nearly still. If you let go of a movement key, your character slides for a moment before stopping. Shooting during that slide is the most common reason a "perfect" shot misses.

Counter-strafing removes the slide. You stop almost instantly, shoot accurately, and can move again before the enemy reacts.

## How to do it

1. Strafe right by holding `key:D`.
2. Release `key:D` and, at the same moment, tap `key:A`. The tap cancels your sideways speed.
3. Fire as soon as you have stopped. Do not hold `key:A`, or you will start moving left and become inaccurate again.
4. Mirror it for the other side: strafe with `key:A`, stop with a tap of `key:D`.

The rhythm is **move, tap, shoot**. The tap is short, about as long as a mouse click.

| Situation | What happens to your shot |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Shooting while strafing | Inaccurate |
| Releasing the key and waiting | Accurate, but slow |
| Counter-strafing | Accurate almost immediately |

## Common mistakes

- **Shooting too early.** You fire during the tap, while you are still moving.
- **Holding the opposite key.** You stop, then drift the other way and miss the second bullet.
- **Only practising one direction.** Most players have a weak side. Find yours and train it twice as much.
- **Crouching instead.** Crouching also steadies your aim, but it leaves you stuck in one place.

## Practice

Use the timing demo below until you can stop and shoot in the green zone five times in a row from each side. Then do the same in an empty server: strafe out from behind a wall, counter-strafe, fire one bullet at a mark on the wall, and strafe back.
