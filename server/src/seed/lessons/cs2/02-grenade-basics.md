## Why it matters

Grenades win rounds that aim alone cannot. They block vision, blind enemies, deny areas with fire and finish off players who are already hurt.

It is tempting to spend everything on weapons and armour, but a full-buy without grenades is half a buy. Knowing what each grenade does, and buying it, makes every round you play more effective.

## How to do it

1. **Know the four types.** A smoke blocks vision for a while. A flashbang blinds anyone looking at it. A molotov or incendiary denies an area with fire. An HE grenade does damage.
2. **Know what you can carry.** You can carry up to four grenades, with only one of each type, except flashbangs, where you can carry two.
3. **Buy utility early.** Buy grenades before upgrading armour or weapons you do not need.
4. **Use smoke against fire.** A smoke also puts out fire, so it can clear a molotov that blocks your path.
5. **Throw flashes out of your own view.** Throw them around a corner or overhead, so they pop where the enemy sees them and you do not.

| Grenade | What it does | Typical use |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| Smoke | Blocks vision for a while | Cutting a sightline before you cross it |
| Flashbang | Blinds anyone looking at it | Right before you or a teammate peeks |
| Molotov / incendiary | Denies an area with fire | Clearing a hiding spot or slowing a push |
| HE grenade | Does damage | Finishing enemies who are already hurt |

## Common mistakes

- Buying a better weapon and no grenades.
- Throwing a flash straight ahead, so it blinds you too.
- Walking through fire instead of smoking it out.
- Dying with all your grenades still unused.

## Practice

Start a practice match on your own and buy four grenades. Throw each type at least once and watch what it does: how long the smoke blocks vision, how a flash affects you when you face it and when you turn away, and how a smoke puts out a molotov. Finish by throwing flashes around a corner and overhead until they pop out of your own view every time.
