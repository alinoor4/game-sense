## Why it matters

The same grenade lands in very different places depending on how you throw it. If you do not control the throw, a smoke meant to block a sightline lands in the wrong spot and leaves your team exposed.

A lineup removes the guessing. When you know exactly where to stand, what to aim at and how to throw, the grenade lands in the same place every time.

## How to do it

1. **Learn the three throws.** `key:Mouse1` is a long throw. `key:Mouse2` is a short underhand throw. Both together give a medium throw.
2. **Add movement on purpose.** Standing, running and jumping throws travel different distances. A lineup always says which one to use.
3. **Remember the three parts of a lineup.** Where to stand, what to aim at, and which throw.
4. **Start with the essentials.** Learn the two or three smokes your team needs every round before anything fancy.
5. **Check where it lands.** On a practice server, turn on the trajectory preview with the command below to see where the grenade lands before you throw.

```
sv_grenade_trajectory_prac_pipreview 1
```

| Throw | Input |
|-------|-------|
| Long | `key:Mouse1` |
| Medium | `key:Mouse1` and `key:Mouse2` together |
| Short underhand | `key:Mouse2` |

## Common mistakes

- Copying a lineup without noting whether it is a standing, running or jumping throw.
- Using `key:Mouse1` for every throw.
- Learning fancy lineups before the basic smokes your team needs.
- Testing new lineups in a real match instead of on a practice server.

## Practice

Set up a practice server with the commands from the "Practice Server Commands" lesson, including the trajectory preview. Pick one smoke your team needs on your favourite map, note where you stand, what you aim at and which throw you use, and repeat it until it lands five times in a row. To see how lineups are recorded, open the Lineup Maps tool in the Tools menu.
