## Why it matters

When you hold the trigger with the AK-47 or the M4, the rifle kicks. If you do nothing, your bullets climb over the head and then drift to the side, and you lose duels you aimed well.

Spray control keeps those bullets on target. Combined with knowing when to tap instead, it turns your rifle from a gamble into a tool you can trust.

## How to do it

1. **Know the shape.** Both rifles kick upwards for the first bullets, then swing sideways.
2. **Pull down, then across.** Pull down smoothly for the first part of the spray. When the swing starts, counter it by moving in the opposite direction.
3. **Learn the first ten bullets first.** Most fights are over by then, so the rest of the magazine matters much less.
4. **Use the AK-47's headshot.** The AK-47 can kill with one headshot, even against a helmet, so tapping and short bursts are very strong with it.
5. **Stand still.** Do not move while you spray. Movement adds inaccuracy on top of the recoil.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling straight down for the whole spray and ignoring the sideways swing.
- Pulling down in jerks instead of smoothly.
- Practising full magazines before the first ten bullets are reliable.
- Spraying with the AK-47 at long range when a tap could kill.
- Strafing while you spray.

## Practice

Set up a private server with the commands from the "Practice Server Commands" lesson, so your bullet impacts are visible. Stand ten metres from a wall and spray ten magazines, switching between the AK-47 and the M4. After each spray, compare the impact marks with where you aimed, and focus on keeping the first ten bullets in a tight group.
