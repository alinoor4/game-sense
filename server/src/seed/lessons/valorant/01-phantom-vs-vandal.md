## Why it matters

The two main rifles trade off differently, and each rewards different habits. Picking the one that matches how you play makes your existing aim count for more.

The choice is not the biggest factor, though. Understanding the trade-off lets you choose once, stop worrying about it and spend your practice time on the fundamentals.

## How to do it

1. **Know the Vandal.** It kills with one headshot at any range. It rewards precise first shots and long sightlines.
2. **Know the Phantom.** It has a faster fire rate and a silencer that hides your bullet tracers, but it loses damage at long range.
3. **Notice where the Phantom is forgiving.** It is easier to control when spraying at close and medium range, and when shooting through smoke.
4. **Pick one and stay with it.** Use the same rifle for a few weeks. Consistency beats switching.
5. **Keep the fundamentals first.** Crosshair placement and stopping before you shoot matter more than which rifle you hold.

| Rifle | Strength | Best for |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Vandal | One headshot kills at any range | Precise first shots and long sightlines |
| Phantom | Faster fire rate, and a silencer that hides tracers | Spraying at close and medium range, and shooting through smoke |

## Common mistakes

- Switching rifles after every lost duel.
- Taking long-range fights with the Phantom, where it loses damage.
- Expecting the Vandal's one-headshot kill to make up for low crosshair placement.
- Blaming the rifle for a shot you took while still moving.

## Practice

Go to the practice range and play five rounds with each rifle. With the Vandal, focus on precise first shots at long-range targets. With the Phantom, spray at close and medium targets and notice how forgiving it feels. Note which rifle felt more natural, then commit to that one for the next few weeks.
