## Why it matters

Shooting while running or jumping makes rifle bullets inaccurate. This is called movement error, and it is a common reason why a shot that looked perfect on your screen still misses.

The first bullet from a standing rifle, on the other hand, is very accurate. If you learn to stop completely before every shot, the shots you aim become the shots that land.

## How to do it

1. **Stop completely before you shoot.** Releasing the movement key stops you quickly. Tapping the opposite key as you release makes the stop more reliable.
2. **Do not trust walking.** Walking with `key:Shift` is quiet, but it is still less accurate than standing.
3. **Crouch with care.** Crouching in the middle of a spray lowers recoil, but it makes you an easier target if you stay there.
4. **Tap at long range.** Because the first bullet from a standing rifle is very accurate, take single taps when the enemy is far away.

| What you are doing | Accuracy of your shot |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Running or jumping | Inaccurate: movement error |
| Walking with `key:Shift` | Quiet, but less accurate than standing |
| Standing still | First bullet very accurate |

## Common mistakes

- Firing while you are still sliding after releasing the key.
- Holding the opposite key too long and starting to move the other way.
- Walking into a fight and shooting without stopping.
- Crouching mid-spray and then staying crouched in the same spot.

## Practice

In the practice range, strafe sideways, stop, and fire one tap at a target, then repeat in the other direction. For ten minutes, alternate between stopping by releasing the key and stopping by tapping the opposite key, and notice which stop feels more reliable.
