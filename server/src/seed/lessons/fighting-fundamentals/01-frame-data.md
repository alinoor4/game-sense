## Why it matters

Fighting games run at 60 frames per second, and every move is measured in frames. Frame data describes how long a move takes to come out, how long it can hit, and how long you are stuck afterwards.

You do not need to memorise numbers to use it. Even a rough idea of which moves are safe tells you when it is your turn to attack and when you should block.

## How to do it

1. **Learn the three phases.** Startup is the time before a move can hit. Active is the time it can hit. Recovery is the time after, when you cannot act yet.
2. **Read on-block advantage.** After a move is blocked, plus means you can act first, and minus means the opponent can.
3. **Spot unsafe moves.** A move that is very minus on block is unsafe: the opponent gets a guaranteed hit, called a punish.
4. **Sort your moves.** Instead of memorising numbers, start by learning which of your moves are safe and which are not.
5. **Punish the opponent's unsafe moves.** When the opponent's move is very minus on block, block it and take the guaranteed hit.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Startup | The frames before a move can hit |
| Active | The frames in which a move can hit |
| Recovery | The frames after a move, when you cannot act yet |
| Plus on block | You can act first after the move is blocked |
| Minus on block | The opponent can act first after the move is blocked |
| Punish | A guaranteed hit against a move that is very minus on block |

## Common mistakes

- Using unsafe moves carelessly and hoping the opponent does not punish.
- Pressing buttons when you are minus instead of blocking.
- Assuming a move is safe because nobody has punished it yet.
- Trying to memorise every number before you play.

## Practice

Pick the moves you use most and try each of them in training mode with the opponent set to block. After each blocked move, notice whether you can act first or whether the opponent can, and sort the moves into a safe list and an unsafe list. In your next matches, use only moves from the safe list whenever you are not sure they will hit.
