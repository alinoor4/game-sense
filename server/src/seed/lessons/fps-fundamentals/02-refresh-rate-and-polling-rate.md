## Why it matters

You can only react to what your screen shows you. A monitor with a higher refresh rate draws new pictures more often, so an enemy who steps out appears sooner and moves more smoothly. That gives your eyes and your hand more to work with.

A fast monitor does nothing if a setting somewhere holds it back. A few quick checks make sure nothing does, and that nothing adds delay.

## How to do it

1. **Understand refresh rate.** Refresh rate, measured in hertz (Hz), is how many times per second the monitor draws a new picture. More Hz means you see movement sooner and smoother.
2. **Enable it in the operating system.** A high-refresh monitor only helps if its rate is actually enabled in the operating system's display settings. Check there, not just on the box.
3. **Keep FPS at or above it.** The game's frames per second (FPS) should be at or above the monitor's refresh rate, or the extra Hz has nothing new to show.
4. **Prefer stable FPS.** Stable FPS matters more than peak FPS. Lower your graphics settings until the frame rate stops dipping in fights, because fights are exactly when you need it.
5. **Leave polling rate at the standard.** Polling rate is how often the mouse reports its position. 1000 Hz is the common standard and is enough.
6. **Cut input delay.** Use fullscreen mode and close background programs to reduce input delay.

| Setting | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| Monitor Hz in the operating system | The monitor's high refresh rate is selected in the display settings |
| In-game FPS cap | The cap is at or above the monitor's Hz |
| Mouse polling rate | It is set to 1000 Hz |
| Fullscreen | The game runs in fullscreen mode |

## Common mistakes

- Plugging in a new monitor and never changing the rate in the display settings.
- Capping FPS below the monitor's refresh rate.
- Chasing the highest possible FPS with settings that drop sharply in fights.
- Leaving downloads, videos and other programs running in the background while you play.

## Practice

Go through the table above today. Open your operating system's display settings and confirm the refresh rate, then check the FPS cap and the window mode in your game. Finally, play a busy practice round while watching your frame counter. If it dips during fights, lower your graphics settings step by step until it holds steady.
