## Why it matters

Your setup decides which muscles do the aiming. If you sit hunched with a tense shoulder, small movements turn shaky and long sessions start to hurt.

A comfortable position you can repeat makes your aim the same every day. It also lets you play longer without pain, which matters more than any setting in the menu.

## How to do it

1. **Rest your forearm on the desk.** Sit so your forearm rests on the desk and your elbow is near a right angle. Keep your shoulders relaxed.
2. **Place the monitor.** Put it at arm's length, with the top of the screen near eye level.
3. **Pick a grip and keep it.** The three common grips are compared below. None of them is "correct"; what matters is using the same grip consistently.
4. **Split the work.** Aim with your arm for large movements and with your wrist and fingers for small corrections.
5. **Check your mouse pad.** It should be large enough for a full 180° turn at your sensitivity without lifting the mouse.
6. **Take breaks.** Stop for a short break every hour. Pain is a signal to stop, not to push through.

| Grip | How you hold the mouse | Strength |
|------|------------------------|----------|
| Palm | Your whole hand rests on the mouse | Stable |
| Claw | Your fingers are arched | Quick clicks |
| Fingertip | Only your fingertips touch the mouse | Most agile, but least stable |

## Common mistakes

- Resting only the wrist on the desk and aiming every movement from the wrist.
- Switching grips every few days.
- Lifting the mouse in the middle of a turn because the pad is too small.
- Playing on through pain in the wrist, arm or shoulder.

## Practice

Before your next session, adjust your chair until your forearm rests on the desk with your elbow near a right angle, and move the monitor to arm's length with the top near eye level. Then stand in an open area of any map and turn a full 180° with a smooth arm movement. If you run out of mouse pad before the turn ends, you need a larger pad. Finally, set a timer so you take a short break every hour you play.
