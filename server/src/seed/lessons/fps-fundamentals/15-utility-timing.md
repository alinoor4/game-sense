## Why it matters

Utility means grenades and abilities that block vision, blind, damage an area or reveal enemies. Used well, it wins fights before a shot is fired. Used badly, it is wasted and only warns the enemy that you are coming.

The difference is almost always timing. The same flash that wins a round when a teammate moves on it does nothing when they arrive late.

## How to do it

1. **Combine utility with a push.** Utility used with a teammate's push is worth far more than utility used alone. Call it, then count down so everyone moves at the same moment.
2. **Move on the flash.** A flash is only good if a teammate moves on it within about a second of it popping.
3. **Smoke the sightline that hurts most.** Smokes buy time and cut sightlines; they do not kill. Decide which sightline hurts you most and block that one.
4. **Keep some for later.** Save some utility for after the objective is taken, or for the retake.
5. **Match the type to the job.** Use the table below to pick what to throw.

| Utility type | What it is for | When to use it |
|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| Vision blocker (smoke) | Buying time and cutting a sightline | Before you cross or take an area that is being watched |
| Blind (flash) | Blinding enemies | Right before a teammate moves |
| Area damage | Clearing or denying a spot | When enemies hide where you cannot easily check, or to slow a push |
| Reveal | Finding enemies | Before you commit to an area |

## Common mistakes

- Throwing a flash and then waiting, so it pops with nobody moving.
- Smoking a sightline that was not a threat while the dangerous one stays open.
- Expecting a smoke to win the fight on its own.
- Using everything in the first ten seconds, so nothing is left for the retake.

## Practice

In your next match, never throw a flash without a teammate ready to move on it: call it, count down, and throw so it pops as they go. In every round, also decide which sightline you would smoke first and why. After the match, note how many rounds you still had utility left after taking the objective or for the retake, and try to raise that number in the next match.
